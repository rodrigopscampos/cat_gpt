**Question 1:** What are the four age-related feline life stages?
**Answer 1:** Kitten, young adult, mature adult, and senior.

---

**Question 2:** How often should senior cats get a routine check-up?
**Answer 2:** At least every 6 months.

---

**Question 3:** What is the formula to calculate a cat’s Resting Energy Requirement (RER)?
**Answer 3:** $\text{RER} = 30 \times (\text{body weight in kg}) + 70$.

---

**Question 4:** How many litter boxes should a home have?
**Answer 4:** One for each cat, plus one extra.

---

**Question 5:** What size should a cat’s litter box be?
**Answer 5:** At least 1.5 times the length of the cat from nose to tail tip.

---

**Question 6:** What core vaccines do all cats need?
**Answer 6:** Rabies, feline herpesvirus (FHV-1), feline calicivirus (FCV), and feline panleukopenia (FPV).

---

**Question 7:** Where on the body should cat vaccines be administered?
**Answer 7:** In the lower legs or the end of the tail, avoiding the area between the shoulders.

---

**Question 8:** Why is anesthesia-free dental cleaning recommended against?
**Answer 8:** It causes stress and injury risk, misses internal dental issues, and only cleans the visible surface.

---

**Question 9:** What percentage of dry matter protein do healthy senior cats need?
**Answer 9:** A minimum of 30% to 45%.

---

**Question 10:** At what age is a cat considered a senior?
**Answer 10:** Over 10 years of age.
